# QuickParcel help centre (fictional sample data for the demo)

## refund-damaged
If your parcel arrives damaged, report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the box and the item. We refund the declared value of the item to the original payment method within 7 working days after the claim is approved.

## refund-lost
If tracking has shown no movement for 10 days, the parcel is treated as lost. We refund the shipping fee and the declared value, up to ₹50,000, after a 3-day search. Claims for lost parcels must be raised within 30 days of booking.

## compensation-late
Late delivery is not refunded. If an Express parcel arrives more than 24 hours after the promised time, you get a voucher worth 50% of the shipping fee. Standard parcels have no late-delivery compensation.

## returns-sender
To return a parcel to the sender before it is delivered, request "Return to sender" in the app. A return fee equal to the original shipping fee is charged, and the parcel goes back within 5 working days.

## returns-ecommerce
Returns of online-store orders are handled by the store, not by QuickParcel. We only carry the return parcel once the store books a reverse pickup for you.

## cod-limits
Cash on Delivery is available for parcels with a declared value up to ₹20,000. The receiver can pay in cash or by UPI to the delivery partner. COD is not available for international shipments.

## cod-remittance
For business accounts, COD amounts collected from receivers are transferred to the sender's bank account every Tuesday and Friday, minus a 1.5% handling charge.

## prohibited-items
We cannot carry cash, gold or silver bullion, lithium batteries shipped on their own, aerosols, perfumes containing alcohol, or any liquids above 500 ml. Batteries are allowed only when installed inside a device.

## international-customs
International parcels need a commercial invoice. Customs duty is paid by the receiver unless the sender chooses "Duties Paid" at booking. Customs clearance usually adds 2 to 4 days.

## international-documents
For international documents (no goods), no invoice is needed and there is no customs duty, but the envelope must weigh under 500 g.

## pickup-schedule
Pickups booked before 2 PM are collected the same day. Pickups booked after 2 PM, on Sundays or on public holidays are collected the next working day.

## pickup-reschedule
You can change a pickup time up to 1 hour before the scheduled slot from the app at no charge. A missed pickup because the sender was unavailable is rescheduled once for free; after that a ₹49 fee applies.

## delivery-attempts
We try to deliver up to 3 times. After the third failed attempt, the parcel waits at the nearest hub for 7 days for self-collection and is then returned to the sender.

## address-change
The delivery address can be changed only within the same city and only before the parcel is "Out for delivery". Changing to a different city requires a new booking.

## weight-disputes
If the weight we measure at the hub is higher than the booked weight, the difference is charged to the sender's wallet. You can dispute the charge within 7 days by sending a photo of the parcel on a scale.

## insurance
Parcels with a declared value above ₹5,000 are insured automatically for 2% of the declared value. Insurance covers loss and damage but not delays.
